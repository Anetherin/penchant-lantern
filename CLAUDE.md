# Notes for Claude

## Architecture & Build
- Plain JS split into classic scripts (shared globals, inline `onclick` handlers). No bundler, no modules.
  Script order is defined in `loader.js` (`FILES`); `tools/check-data.mjs` reads the same list. Add new files there.
- Every top-level name is global across files; avoid name clashes (grep before adding).
- Editable text/numbers live in `data/`; `loader.js` (`DATA_FILES`) fetches them into `window.PL_DATA` before any
  script runs (the game runs from `loader.js` on GitHub Pages; there is no single-file build any more). New data set → add to `DATA_FILES` (and `DATA_DIRS` if split). Keep `_readme` keys current.
- Split data sets (`DATA_DIRS` in `loader.js`: `characters`, `guests`, `walks`, `arcs`, `afterstories`, `housestories`, `flavor`, `firsts`, `goddess`, `traits`, `guild`) live in folders: `data/<name>/index.json` lists parts, deep-merged in order (objects by key, arrays concatenated; a scalar defined twice fails `tools/check-data.mjs`). Node tools read data through `tools/data.mjs` (`loadData(name)`, `partFiles`, `allDataFiles`). Run `node tools/fmt-json.mjs` after editing data files programmatically.
- House chapters keep their logic (checks, costs, effects) in `js/stories/houses.js` / `house-extras.js` / `ngplus-houses.js`; all their text is in `data/housestories/` (one entry per chapter in play order: `pages` = lead-in shown as `ev.pre`, `hint`, `text`, `choices.<id>.{label, res | win/lose, lead...}`; per part `titles`, shared lines in `base.json → common`), read with `HT(h, chapter, 'path', vars)` / `HC(path, vars)` (houses.js; chapter indices are hard-coded in the calls). Encounter choices stay in JS. Random events keep logic in `js/events/random.js` and wording in `data/events.json` via `ET(...)`. Partner arcs (`data/arcs/`) and after-stories (`data/afterstories/`) use a declarative effect schema resolved by `resolveEff()` in `js/stories/partners.js`; bond events (`data/bonds.json`; requirements include `key`/`job`/`race` per lady and `pair` for named pairs) use `applyBondChoice()` in `js/events/bonds.js`; walk events (`data/walks.json`) use `applyWalkOutcome()` in `js/events/walks.js`.
- Save state version is `S.v = 43`; migrations live in `migrate()` (`js/logic/state.js`). Bump `S.v` when changing save shape. Only the last few versions convert (`SAVE_MIN`, 36): older saves are refused (`saveTooOld`) rather than migrated, so drop migration blocks that fall below it.
- `NAMED_LOOK` fields (`name`, `race`, `sub`, `age`, `design`, `body`, `style`, `favColor`) are stripped from saves/exports by `saveReplacer` (`store.set(k, v, rep)`) and refilled from data by `namedLook()` in `ensureWorker`, so data edits reach existing saves. Generic ladies keep theirs in the save.
- No Creator tab (`LIB` templates still feed the candidate pool in `refreshCandidates`).

## Writing & Image Prompts
- Adding a partner, house, story/guild lady or guest: follow `docs/CONTENT.md` (every file to touch, typical lengths).
- Scene text follows `docs/WRITING.md`: second-person narration ("you" = Sam; "Sam" stays in dialogue, UI/logs/reports, and AI/image payloads), vivid direct explicit wording with natural anatomical variety (avoiding fade-to-black euphemisms and preserving dialogue/character beats), and wording that matches the service played. All characters are adults.
- Image prompts are built programmatically (`js/logic/prompts.js`). Intimate scenes (`isSvcScene`) lead with `scenes.json → people` head-count tags, pose, and service snippet, then `charDesc(w)` (`bodyDesc(w)` merges `design.build` + `body` into one phrase), clothing (`clothText`, `pubicText`), expression, and setting. `design.accessories` + `outfit.accessories` respect `w.accState` (`dressed`/`under`/`always`/`off`); `design.features` stays visible (`featureSkip` excludes non-visual tags).
- Bath scenes (`bath:true` / `towel:true` / `~~bath`) use the bathhouse; only `bath:true` outfits stay on. Swim scenes (`swim:true`) keep `bath:true` outfits on without a towel. `undress` sets invitation dress level; those posed scenes (`teaseScene`: `undress` or `tease:true`, not robe/bath/mishap/services) use `snippets.json → clothing.*_tease` wording plus a random `poses.tease` pose instead of the dishevelled `partial`. Sam scenes (`samact:`/`duo:`) roll a dress level per moment via `samSceneCloth`. Story moments can define `{comp, setting, sam}` → `dynScene(key, img)` (`js/data/scenes.js`); `image.cloth` fixes the dress level the text describes and `image.wear` replaces her outfit wording (and its look overrides) for that moment (`sceneWear` in `getOutfit`). Off-duty Sam scenes (`sam:true` without `house:true`) never use job uniforms.
- Prompt assembly (`js/logic/prompts.js`): hair/eyes/skin go through `nounPhrase`/`hairText` ("crimson hair, loose waves", "black hair with glowing gold tips"); average body options have no wording (`body.json` img ""); services with `image.bust` put her bust next to the pose (`bustText`) instead of in her description; `exprText` drops general mood pieces that repeat the service's own face wording; `dedupeSegs` removes repeated segments per person block (whole prompt when she's alone). In duo/pair scenes the other woman is as dressed as the scene at most (`matchCloth`), both women are labelled (`personBlock`: first/second woman, the woman/the other woman, the futanari), the futanari's look reads "futanari" instead of "woman" (`charDesc` opt `futa`), and the second woman leaves out accessories (`noAcc`). Two women in one picture (`pair:`/`cpair:`, and `duo:` threesomes) never show outfits: each is in her underwear or nude (`pairScene`/`pairCloth`, `scenes.json → pairCloth`, `futaPhraseUnder`). A woman client (`cpair:`) is one short phrase up front like a man client (`scenes.json → femClientPhrase`/`futaClientPhrase`, a hair colour unlike the lady's), and the lady's block is labelled "the woman". Pose wording (`services.json → image.duo/fem/futa`) names people by the prompt's labels ("the first woman"/"the second woman", "the woman"/"the other woman", "the futanari"), the active one first. Double dates are pictured as `ddate_<date>~~with:<other id>` (`ddScene` in dates.js, `ddatePrompt` in prompts.js): both women and Sam, each labelled as `image.comp` names them. Every woman in a prompt is called adult (group-night extras and clients included): Flux-based models ignore the negative prompt, so the positive prompt carries that.
- Prompt audit: `node tools/prompt-audit.mjs [--seed N] [--only pair_futa,duo] [--json f]` builds every scene family across a cast covering every body option, race, dress level, the goddess and a spirit, lints them (man/penis leaks in two-women scenes, missing labels, repeats, dress clashes, length) and writes `tools/prompt-audit.out.md`. Run it after touching prompts.js, scenes.js prompt code, services.json `image`, snippets, body or scenes data.
- Two women together: `pair:<service>:<fem|futa|selffuta>:<other id>` (`futa` = the other one is the futanari, e.g. the goddess; `selffuta` = she is, e.g. a spirit). Futa poses (`services.json → image.futa`) name {name} "the woman" and {partner} "the futanari"; the prompt labels both and describes the futanari first. Reverse possession with Sam (`sam_spirit_futa`) or a client (`spirit_futa`) is pictured solo (`people.solo_futa`).
- Image gallery (`js/ui/gallery.js`, Settings → 🖼 Browse images or 🖼 Gallery on a sheet): Checklist view runs her `sheetScenes` for one outfit (`galLady` stand-in), expression (client services as `act:<id>~<expr>~`) and dress level, marking saved/missing; All saved lists her keys (local + cloud manifest) by outfit (`o:<outfit>` in the key). Generation queues through a floating tray (`#galTray`, `GAL_GEN`) outside the modal; a picture in another outfit never replaces her avatar.
- AI text is opt-in per scene (✨ button; `SET.ai.auto` defaults off, except `autoBackstory` for generics). `{outcome}` comes from `aiOutcome()` + `ai-prompts.json → outcomes`; `actSituation()` supplies service context.

## Stats, Effects, Traits & Titles
- Lady stats (`STAT_KEYS`, 8 total: `beauty`, `charm`, `charisma`, `refinement`, `wits`, `stamina`, `strength`, `agility`), `tech` skills, and `acts[id].skill` run `0-1000` (`SKL = 10`, `s100(v)`, `clampK`); data files stay `0-100` and convert at creation (`genWorker`, `makeNamed`). Stat cap is `statCap()` (`ngplus.cap.base + perCycle * (cycle - 1)`), growth curve denominator `growDen() = statCap() + 300`; never hard-code `1000`/`1300`. See `docs/STATS.md` for weights and formulas.
- Stat bonus layer (`js/logic/fx.js`): `w.stats` holds trained base stats; race/sub `mods` (except `lewd`), `fx.stats`, and worn outfit/underwear `stats` add live via `statOf(w, k)` / `statBonus(w, k)`. Every gameplay read must use `statOf(w, k)`.
- `SAM_STATS` (5 total, `0-100`, ceiling `samCap() = statCap() / 10`): `charm`, `business`, `strength`, `wits`, `technique`. Passive bonuses via `samBonus(k, stat)` (`balance.json → sam.bonus`); max energy `samMaxEnergy()`, +1 AP every 5 levels (`apMax()`).
- Effects engine (`js/logic/fx.js`): `fxMul/fxAdd/fxHas/fxSub/fxArr` merge `data/traits/` (`general`, `earnable`, `ngperks`), `data/races.json`, `data/body.json`, `w.unique`, `w.ngPerks`, and active title (`w.titleId`). Vocabulary is in `data/traits/base.json`. Developable traits (`earn`) check weekly via `earnCheck(w)` against `w.counts` (`countUp`).
- **Week = rest day + six work nights.** `simHouse` runs night by night (`balance.json → nights`, `props.nights`): each night an escort takes up to `nightCapOf(w)` clients (1 + Stamina/`perStamina`, + `nightCap` fx; demons +1) while energy lasts; staff offers and restless encounters are spread over the nights (`splitShare`); then everyone sleeps (`nightRest`: bed x `restMult` x `rest.night`, a little Health wear `rest.wear.nightBase/nightPerSpent`). `endWeek` adds the rest day (`rest.restDay`) or a week off (`rest.week`). Crowding is judged per night against the weekly `crowdCap`. Sam's actions are rest-day actions.
- Energy (`balance.json → rest`): max energy = `maxBase + maxPerStamina x Stamina` (Stamina = how much), recovery x Strength (`restMult`, Strength = how fast); services cost their flat `energy` (no Stamina discount). Wear (`fatigueCut`/`wornDown`, dwarves' `fatigue` fx) cuts recovery; a week off or a Garden heals it. Auto-rest keeps `w.prevJob` (shown via `jobOptions`/`jobLabelOf`, holds her position in `canDo`, a resting madame keeps her courtesan slots in `slotsFor`). Androids charge to full every night (fee at the week's end); demons feed instead.
- Service labels: `label` is her side (client menus, reports); `samLabel` (`samActLabel(id)`) is Sam's side for his own menus/summaries (private time, practice, threesomes, goddess nights).
- Service ids: `ACT_ALIAS` in `config.js` maps old `caress`/`manual`/`oral`/`paizuri` → `kiss`/`manual_give`/`oral_give`/`breast_give`. Two-way services are `_give`/`_get` pairs.
- Trust & Lewdness stay `0-100` internally; UI displays ×10 (`tShow()`, `tDelta()`, `lShow()`). `minorTrust(w)` is the minimum trust floor for time with Sam. Lewdness gains go through `lewdUp(w, n, cap)`; some rubs off weekly via `lewdInfluence` (staff.js, `balance.json → lewd.influence`: madames from their courtesans, attendants from theirs, floor staff from exhibitionists).
- Titles (`js/logic/titles.js`, `data/titles.json`): `w.name` never includes an honorific; display with `titled(w)` on cards/sheets/pickers. Active title `w.titleId` grants its `fx` (`syncTitles`). Earned via seed, weekly `titleCheck`, `TITLE_EVENTS`, or story/arc completion (`awardTitles`). Tiers: a title's `from` lists what it upgrades; `giveTitle` replaces those (one title per line, `syncTitles` drops any she has grown past), earn titles with `from` need one first (`titleReady`, unless `open`). Lineage helpers: `titleLine`, `titleAbove`, `titleTier`, `holdsTitle` (use it instead of checking `w.titles` directly). Retired ids map via `titles.json → renamedTitles`.

## Houses, Jobs, Clients & Simulation
- Weekly sim is split across `js/logic/services.js` (client selection, quality/expressions, `runService`/`serveAct`, escalations, `pushyCheck`), `js/logic/staff.js` (classroom teaching, `attendantPairs`, `syncAttendants`), `js/logic/week.js` (`simHouse`, `updatePrestige`, `nightIncidents`, `nightRest`), `js/logic/endweek.js` (`endWeek`), and `js/ui/report.js` (`showReport`, `REPV`: `all` / `h:<house>` / `w:<lady>` with `repWhy` & `repGroups`).

## Progression, NG+, NG++ & Goddess
- **Debt (`balance.json → debt`):** `nextDue()` uses `debtShares()` (ramped payments, no balloon) from `S.debt.start`; `payOffDebt()` / `payExtra()` trigger `checkEnd()`.
- **New Game+ (`js/logic/ngplus.js`):** Home house always stays; `S.starters` and finished-story houses qualify (`keepable`, toggled on `NGS.keep`). Kept houses keep upgrades/story and carry `improveShare` (1) of `h.spent` plus `keptHouseShare` into debt; `ngNumbers` adds `loverDebt` + `perLover` scaling and new-house debt. Devoted lovers carry over (draw wages, pick one `ngPerk`, optional `ngRomance` reset); kept-house residents/story-joiners return reincarnated; max-trust guests & favorited generics become `S.alumni` (`inviteFriend`). Everyone carried gains a `w.reborn` stack and keeps `carryShare(w)` of gains above base; `S.lives` remembers departed named ladies (`rebirthNamed`), including epilogue progress and their romance (`romanceOf`/`restoreRomance`: arc, firsts, Devoted; partners only outside NG++, all cleared once at the first NG++). Carried/alumni bonds scale by `ngplus.bonds.keep`. Cycle 2+ unlocks `ngplus: true` houses/partners and optional `CYCLE_CHALLENGES` (`S.challengePerks`; `within` = schedule share, `mods`/`adds` apply all cycle and `perkMods`/`perkAdds` for good through `townMul`/`townAdd`; cycle-long goals tracked weekly in `S.chTrack` by `challengeTrack()`, checked by `challengeMet()` in random.js; Tight Troupe's cap `troupeCap()` scales with carried lovers/kept houses). `ngSeed()` exports a clean replayable NG+ seed; the cloud snapshot (`cloudSaveMilestone`, slot `<run>_c<cycle>_ngp`) is taken at payoff and refreshed at `ngBegin`, before the rebirth.
- **Lingering (`js/logic/linger.js`, `balance.json → linger`, wording `goddess/linger.json → linger`):** after payoff (`S.wonWk`) and a grace period, each week is a linger level: `wane` (Sam's max energy, `lingerEnergyCut`), `tithe` (share of gold above a floor), `recall` (every few weeks a Devoted lover who isn't `S.partner` moves to `S.lingerTaken` until `startNGPlus` → `lingerWake()`; `ngSeed` carries them). Each stage opens with a goddess scene in her current voice (dream / known / bound / gone); Sam tab shows `lingerCard()`. `paidGap(g)` shortens story waits (arcs, epilogues, house chapters, firsts) by `linger.waitMul` once the debt is paid. Lab: `patch.balance.linger.off`.
- **Town events (`townEventWeek` / `evTownEvent` in `js/events/random.js`, `balance.json → townEvents`, wording `events.json → townEvents`):** in every cycle, every `gap` weeks (first after `firstWeek`, reset per cycle by `townEvReset`) a 2-3 week event runs (`S.townEv {id, start, end, house, overrides}`, `S.townEvNext`); announcement popups offer interactive choices (`choices` with gold/rep/mood/overrides/early cancel). Multipliers apply through `townEvFor(h)` → `townMul(k, def, h)` / `townAdd(k, def, h)` (`singleHouse: true` events target one house only; `random: false` covers Isolde's `price_war_*` and `wager_won`/`wager_lost`), and the header shows `townEvChip()`. Lab: `patch.balance.townEvents.off` / `.only`.
- **Isolde (`js/events/isolde.js`, `data/isolde.json`, `balance.json → isolde`):** rival storyline, `S.isolde {stage, wk, best, desire, nights, truce, lovers, rivalWk, rivalSeen...}`; `isoldeWeek(R)` in `endWeek` queues the next beat (`ISO_BEATS`: taunt, visit, pricewar → `startTownEvent('price_war_match'|'price_war_hold')`, wager → `wager_won`/`wager_lost`, truce, lovers), standing-based rival events (`isoStanding()` `ahead`/`neck`/`behind` → `evIsoldeRival`), her visits (`evIsoldeNight('comes')`) and the Thorn's overflow gold; Sam visits on the rest day (`isoVisit`). Nights with Isolde trigger `goddessIsoldeCheck` (`evGoddessIsolde` pre-binding intercept or bound demon/jealous reclamation). Nights picture `npc_isolde` as `samact:<act>~~iso_<night id>` (`isoSetting`); the approach decides the position (`approach.<yield|take|contest>.act`: she's on top / from behind / face to face; a lost take plays yield) and the night's `finish.<approach>` text. Tone: the rawest scenes in the game, rivalry and lust rather than romance: aggressive and physical (no real injuries), with her filthy talk written as dialogue. She never joins. Her story carries over unfinished into NG+ (`isoNewCycle()` only restarts it in NG++, every NG++ cycle; texts by `isoTier()`: `ngpp.deja` / `pull` / `hungry`); NG+ cycles after the one it finished in get her games (`evIsoldeGame`, `isolde.json → games`, `balance.json → isolde.games`). Poaching (random.js) stops at the truce.
- **Goddess & NG++ (`js/logic/goddess.js`, `docs/GODDESS.md`):** Seliora's teases run cycles 1–5 (`evGoddess`, `evGoddessDistract`, walks); binding happens on rebirth once a lady exceeds `fullReborn`. Bound Seliora gets her own tab (`GFOCUS` includes `clients` and `focus` backup, plus jealous `evGoddessIsolde` reactions to Isolde). Once a lady hits `fullReborn` and Seliora is bound/binding, NG+ enters **NG++** at The Lantern Shrine (`shrine`) with a **Bound Vessel**, soft-resetting Sam/houses/lovers while keeping `S.boons`, `S.pasts`, `S.challengePerks`, `S.goddess`, raised caps, `S.vessels`, and `S.lives` / `S.alumni`.
