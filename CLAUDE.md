# Notes for Claude

- Game is plain JS split into classic scripts (shared globals, inline `onclick` handlers). No bundler, no modules.
  Script order is defined in `loader.js` (FILES); `tools/build.mjs` reads the same list. Add new files there.
- Every top-level name is global across files; avoid name clashes (grep before adding).
- Editable text/numbers live in `data/*.json`; `loader.js` (DATA_FILES) fetches them into `window.PL_DATA` before any
  script runs, and `tools/build.mjs` inlines them. New data file → add it to DATA_FILES. Keep `_readme` keys current.
  Logic-heavy content (house stories, encounter choices) stays in JS. Random events keep their logic in
  `js/events/random.js` but take all wording from `data/events.json` via `ET(...)`.
  Partner arcs (`data/arcs.json`) use a small declarative effect schema, resolved by `resolveEff()` in `js/stories/partners.js`.
  Bond events and shared scenes (`data/bonds.json`) are resolved by `applyBondChoice()` in `js/events/bonds.js`.
  Crossover/visiting ladies live in `data/guests.json` (packs, merged into NAMED as role 'drifter' by `charData()` in
  `js/core/config.js`); keys must stay unique across characters.json and guests.json.
  Run `node tools/fmt-json.mjs` after editing data files programmatically.
- Image prompts are always built programmatically (`js/logic/prompts.js`). The user writes any explicit wording Claude is uncomfortable with
  themselves in `data/snippets.json` / `data/ai-prompts.json`; All characters are adults;
- Firsts with Sam: `js/events/firsts.js` + `data/firsts.json`. Every named lady AND every guest has her own events (1/2/3, guests also `jump`; `firstData` prefers `events.jump` when she has no firsts yet and the stage is past a kiss); guests' pace/path/init stay rolled. Spicy date `lead` may be a list (picked at random). `canSam` = comfort (`samStance`) AND crossed (`actTier(id) <= w.intim`).
  Every path that leads to private time (dates, encounters, arcs, bonds) goes through `canSam`/`bestSamAct`, so keep it that way.
  Story moments can carry their own image `{comp, setting, sam}` → `dynScene(key, img)` (js/data/scenes.js).
- Job preferences are per lady (`w.jobLikes`/`w.jobDislikes`; named ones from `jobs` in characters/guests.json, others rolled by `rollJobPrefs`).
- New Game+ lives in `js/logic/ngplus.js` (tuning: `balance.json → ngplus`). Sam always keeps his home house; his starter houses (`S.starters`: the first house plus the new house taken each cycle) and any house whose story is finished qualify (`keepable(h)`, `houseStoryDone(h)`) and are optional carries, chosen on the "Houses" step (`NGS.keep`, default all kept, `ngKeep(id)`; `keptHouse(h)` consults it), with their upgrades and story (reputation goes back to `HOUSES[id].rep` + `ngplus.carry` x what it had gained) (`h.spent` tracks improvement
  spending, new debt = home debt share + `spent` of ALL kept houses x `improveShare`); every kept house's resident is recreated if not hired; other houses are surrendered (`saleValue` x cost reduces the debt; no gold carries over: purse = `ngplus.purse` + 2,500 with the purse boon).
  Sam keeps his level but (`ngplus.samReset`) his stats are rebuilt from `sam.base` + `sam.perLevel` x (lvl-1) + `PASTS[k].fx` for each past, and Appraisal restarts. Only Devoted lovers carry over (wages again, stats reset, popularity 0,
  each picks one `ngPerk` trait stored in `w.ngPerks`; trust always resets, and on the perks step the player picks per lover (`NGS.romance`, `ngRomance(C,w)`, default keep) whether she stays his lover or her romance starts over, `ngRomanceReset`: no Devoted, arc 0, `intim` 0, dates/privateCount 0, Oblivious back if in her data);
  residents of kept houses who were working for Sam come back with their house reincarnated (`ngResidents()`, `ngReset` + a `reborn` stack, no perk, a story-freed one stays wage-free; the NG+ save keeps residents of every keepable house); guests and generics at max trust become "old friends" (`ngFriends()`, parked in `S.alumni`, Reincarnated but no perk, turn up as EXTRA candidates via `inviteFriend()`; `balance.json → ngplus.friends`); everyone else leaves and named ladies return to their pools. Bonds between two reincarnated ladies (lovers, old friends, waiting alumni) carry over at `ngplus.bonds.keep` x their value with their kind; `b.past` counts lives survived (sheet: "(from a past life)"). Houses with `ngplus: true` in economy.json
  and partners with `ngplus: true` in characters.json only appear from cycle 2 (`houseOffered` / `partnerOffered`); house stories are in `js/stories/ngplus-houses.js`.
  Debt (`balance.json → debt`): `nextDue()` gives ramped payments (`debtShares()`, sum to total, last clears the rest, no balloon), counted from `S.debt.start` (`debtWeek()`); `payOffDebt()` pays it all at once (same win flow via `checkEnd`).
- House ladies (role `house`) are gated by their house story: `houseGate(w)` / `capTrust(w)` cap trust at `base` + `perBeat` x beat (`balance.json → houseGate`, also `gap`/`retry` waits via `storyGap`/`storyRetry`), enforced in
  `gainTrust`, endWeek and `save()`. Clothes: outfits Sam buys go to the shared `S.houseWardrobe` (underwear: `S.houseUnder`); use `ownsOutfit/ownsUnder`, never `w.owned` alone;
  `giftHouseItem()` gives a piece to a lady (counts as a gift); prices go through `outfitCost()` (`balance.json → wardrobe`).
- Stats, `tech` and service skills run 0-1000 (`SKL`, `s100(v)` back to the old 0-100 scale, `clampK`); data files stay 0-100 and
  are converted at creation (`genWorker`, `makeNamed`). Anything that reads a stat/skill in a formula goes through `s100()`.
- Effects engine: `js/logic/fx.js` (`fxMul/fxAdd/fxHas/fxSub/fxArr`) merges effects from traits (`data/traits.json`), race + beastkin
  sub (`data/races.json`), body (`data/body.json`), `w.unique` and `w.ngPerks`. Vocabulary is in the `_readme` of traits.json.
  Developable traits (`earn`) are checked weekly from `w.counts` (`countUp(w,key)`); old service ids are aliases (`ACT_ALIAS` in config.js: caress→kiss, manual→manual_give, oral→oral_give,
  paizuri→breast_give); two-way services are `_give`/`_get` pairs. Lewdness (and `th`, `dress.fullAt`, `lewdMin`) stays 0-100.
- Jobs (docs/OVERHAUL.md §5-6): `companion`/`reception` are gone (migrated to courtesan/promoter). Courtesan, dancer and bath are `must` (economy.json jobs): they serve Host (`clients.host`, no amenity, slots base 1 + 1 per 4 bedrooms): weekly capacity = perWeek x (1 + service/100 x skillPer x 10) x outputMult, minus `propCost` per accepted proposition; each waiting client costs 1 (entertained: +`host.sat`, cancels `wait.sat`), a warm-up for clients with openness (0.3 + `open` traits) >= `fluffOpen` costs 1 each (light services from her menu, never finished: no `runService`, lust `host.lust`, tip to `led.host`), and `acceptHost` clients may be finished (`runService`, `finishShare` of the fee, `finishCost`, skips the courtesan). Attendants are coached by the madame like courtesans (`madameCoach`) but only need her to be at least as popular (gap 0).
  propositions from their menu; every other role accepts by `propDecide()` (week.js: lust, `w.offDuty` on/ask/off) and loses 8% output per accepted proposition (`outputMult`).
  Tunables are in balance.json (`jobs`, `rooms`, `tidy`, `rep`, `props`). Rooms: `h.rooms x 8` sessions/week (`X.roomOK/useRoom` in `simHouse`); `h.tidy` is kept by maids.

  Reputation gains use `repAdd(h, n)` (scaled by `repGain`); `w.samples` (promoter) is set from the sheet. The madame has no focus options: she matches clients, shields moods and coaches courtesans she out-ranks (`madameCoach`). Attendant slots = courtesan slots; a courtesan with her own attendant gets mood + trust (`ownAtt` in simHouse).
- Trust stays 0-100 internally and in data, but the UI shows it x10 (out of 1000): use `tShow(v)` / `tDelta(n)` (util.js) for any trust number shown to the player.
  `minorTrust(w)` (balance.json → trust.minor) is the floor every activity with Sam builds. Lewdness also stays 0-100 internally but is shown x10 (`lShow`); trust and lewdness are stat cells, not bars (bars: energy, mood, lust, health).
- Sam's house-wide actions: Make the rounds (trust/mood for everyone) and classes (stat class, skill class, demonstration; see the training note); one-on-one training covers her stats, skills and services (samTrain). Sam's release scales with Technique (`samRelease`).
- Lust: giving services build lust only with Sam (with clients only with the `svcLustBuild` fx). Ladies who come to Sam restless use `releaseSamAct` (services that spend lust); `wantsAgain`/`evAgain` offer another round (beastkin most). Demons burn `svc.demon.hunger` energy each working week.
- NG+ extras: each cycle Sam takes a new house (`NGS.house`, debt x newHouseShare) and may add a past life (`S.pasts`; use `hasPast(k)` / `samPasts()` / `pastPick(map)`, never `S.past`).
  Carried ladies gain `w.reborn` stacks (Reincarnated, `rebornFx` in fx.js, balance.json → ngplus.reborn) and reset trust/lewdness to where they began (`w.base0` for generics). The new house's full debt (x perCycle) joins the loan.
