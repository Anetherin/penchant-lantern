# The goddess (Seliora, the Lamplighter)

The overarching story. Code: `js/logic/goddess.js`. Story wording, her look, forms, teases and dedicated intros: `data/goddess/` (`base.json`, `teases.json`, `intros.json`, `partner-intros.json`). Her remarks about houses and past lives: `economy.json → houses[id].goddess`, `pasts[k].goddess`; about partners: `characters/partners.json → <key>.goddess.comment`, and her aside after each partner intro (`goddess/partner-intros.json → <key>.goddess`: how she arranged the meeting). Her lines with ladies: `services.json → pair.fem/futa`; with Sam: `flavor/sam.json → private_act`. Numbers: `data/balance.json → goddess`.
Test: `node tools/goddess-smoke.mjs [weeks per cycle]` (plays to the binding, every focus, nights until her ending).

## Arc
- **Cycle 1:** she brings Sam over. Her intro plays before Marguerite's welcome and comments on his past life. After the partner and house intros she gets an aside of her own (shown to the player; Sam doesn't remember her yet): how she arranged the meeting with his partner, and what she thinks of the house.
- **Every rebirth (NG+ / NG++):** a scene before the Vane contract. It reacts to how fast he paid, which lovers he brought, a new past life, the new house and the new partner, plus a jealousy line.
- **Before the binding (cycles 1-5):** weekly chance of an event (`events.base` + `events.perCycle` × (cycle-1), max `events.max`, plus overdue `catchUp`), and she can also turn up during Sam's walks (`js/events/walks.js`, `data/walks.json → goddess`). She mostly turns up **in disguise**: another race's form with an alias, always with the same hair, eyes and skin. The lantern-dream fades when Sam wakes, so at first he doesn't recognize her.
  - Stranger (cycles 1-2): a strange girl flirts, kisses and gets handsy, then vanishes before he finishes.
  - Suspicion (2-3): he notices the pattern.
  - Reveal (3): he calls her by name, which sets `g.knows`.
  - Game (3-5): the disguises become roleplay.
  - True-form scenes (3-5): sincere and intimate, but she always stops him.
  - Disguise scenes run over pages: `intro` (her arrival, its own page), then `wonder` (Sam's thoughts) with the choices. A choice with `resIn` can turn into her **distracting** him: `resIn` leads in, then a second event page (`evGoddessDistract`) shows a service on Sam with its own picture (`samact:<service>~~gt_<tease id>`, in her disguise, at the tease's setting) and an ending that leaves him wanting (kisses) or high and dry (anything further). How far she goes follows the cycle (`balance.json → goddess.tease.tierByCycle`: kisses/touching, then hands & mouth, then everything, never a finish); the services per tier are `tease.acts`, the odds per phase `tease.chance` (stranger 50%, suspicion and the reveal always: that's when she needs him distracted). Wording: `goddess.json → distract` (lead by phase, acts by service, dry by tier, button by tier); teases/choices may override `act`, `lead`, `dry`, `distract`.
  - Events are `teases`: one-offs run in order; `repeat` ones fill the gaps once every unlocked story tease is seen (`gFresh` / `gOverdue`). `form` is disguise/true/a form key; `unknown`/`needKnows` gate on the reveal.
  - Jealous remarks start at `remarksFrom` (cycle 3). Love comes from your answers. Jealousy grows with new hires, ex-lovers met on walks, private time with others, roster size and lovers carried.
- **The binding:** at the first rebirth after any lady has lived a fully reincarnated life (`w.reborn > fullReborn`, default 4, so the start of cycle 6 at the earliest). She lets him finish, loses her divinity (`divinity.bindTo`) and is bound to him.
- **After the binding:** she gets her own tab.
  - You set a focus (Indulge / Business / Watch / Tutelage / Guild / Work the house (`clients`) / Back Sam up (`focus`) / Her whim), form (`shape`: herself / another race's form / as she pleases) and body option (`form`: fem / futa / as she pleases). She takes 1 action per week, plus 1 per 25 divinity.
  - When jealous she sometimes "claims" Sam or a new lady instead. When unhappy she ignores your guidance.
  - **Work the house (`clients`):** she takes select high-paying clients herself on a house floor in her chosen form, earning gold directly into the ledger (`R.led.goddess` / `R.godClients`) and house reputation.
  - **Back Sam up (`focus`):** she mirrors Sam's weekly focus (`office`, `floor`, `watch`, `guild`, `classes`, `rest`), stacking business/watch/floor satisfaction/trust/skill/energy recovery onto his week.
  - **Isolde jealousy (`goddessIsoldeCheck` / `evGoddessIsolde`):** sleeping with Isolde (`isoNightResult` / `evIsoldeGame` / `evIsoldeLovers`) triggers a pre-binding intercept (`isolde_pre` once `g.met`) or a bound jealous confrontation (`first` in her demon form, then rotating `repeat` scenes `isolde_demon`, `isolde_bath`, `isolde_desk` on cooldown) where she reclaims Sam directly.
  - Her power scales with divinity and with fully reincarnated ladies (and their extra lives).
  - With the ladies (`indulge`) she takes over any tier 1–3 service where she has a pair text (`services.json → pair.fem/futa`), and the lady practices that service.
  - Nights with Sam: pick a service; she does it as herself (the usual private-time lines with her name, image `samact:<act>` with her as the woman).
    - Finishing inside her (a finishing tier-3 service) restores Sam's energy and raises divinity, love and mood, and cuts jealousy.
    - Finishing outside her gives half the energy and a little love, but she sulks.
    - No finish costs mood and love.
  - Neglect: from `neglect.after` (3) weeks without a night she loses love and mood every week. A jealous claim on a lady that Sam doesn't answer with a night within `neglect.claimWeeks` (2) costs extra.
- **Ending:** at full divinity she stays if her love is at least `love.stay` (70) and keeps her full power. Otherwise she leaves for good. A player who keeps coming to her ends well; one who only uses her for divinity and ignores her doesn't.
- **NG++ Soft Reset & Bound Vessels:** starting New Game+ once a lady has lived a full reincarnated life (`w.reborn >= fullReborn`) and Seliora is bound (or binding) enters NG++: houses, savings, lovers, and Sam's level reset cleanly while keeping `S.boons`, `S.pasts`, `S.challengePerks`, `S.goddess`, raised caps, `S.vessels`, and all prior characters' `reborn` stacks and `ngPerks` (in `S.lives` / `S.alumni`). Sam starts at The Lantern Shrine (`shrine`, room cap `10 + 5 * (ngppCycle - 1)`, +1.5% client fees, +2% trust & skill growth, and +1% house popularity gain per reincarnation stack of everyone actively working there, up to 50) alongside a chosen **Bound Vessel** (`createBoundVessel`): empty at first (no personality, dormant mood/lust, job trust capped at 20 until stage 1), awakening across three stages (`vessel.fills`: `[2, 5, 9]`, Stirring → Quickening → Awakened) via `fillVessel` when Sam finishes inside her (`insideFill: 2`, `outsideFill: 1`, `teaseFill: 0.5`). Prior lovers encountered on walks use `S.lives` / `exLoverOpeners` (with 3-tier `reMet` memory lines in `intros.json`), charge no signing bonus, and raise Seliora's jealousy (`perExLover`).
- **Lingering after the debt** (`js/logic/linger.js`, `balance.json → linger`, `goddess/linger.json`): staying on after payoff is fine for a while (grace: 8 weeks, 1 fewer per cycle, at least 4), then the life starts to gutter. Each week past grace is a linger level: Sam's max energy shrinks (wane), she burns a growing share of the gold above a floor (tithe), and from level 4 every 3 weeks she puts a Devoted lover who isn't this cycle's partner to sleep in the lantern until the rebirth (recall; they still carry over). Each stage opens with a scene in her current voice: a dream he can't place (before the reveal), herself, bound, or the lantern alone once she's left. New Game+ wakes everyone and resets it.
- **Challenges:** each NG+/NG++ cycle lets Sam accept an optional `CYCLE_CHALLENGES` rule (`S.challenge`) for a permanent `S.challengePerks` reward on debt payoff (`checkEnd`).
- **Backstory:** she claims she picked Sam on a whim. In truth she had watched him for years in his old world (the stray cat). It slips out in the later teases and comes out at the binding.

## State
`S.goddess` `{met, bound, boundCycle, love, jealousy, mood, divinity, focus, form, shape, autoShape, teases[], evWk, roster, ending, left, nights, finishes, last[], lastNight, claimWk, knows, lastDisguise}`. Save v41 (also tracks `ngppCycle`, `ngppHouses`, `challenge`, `challengePerks[]`, `vessels[]`). It carries through NG+ and NG++ untouched.
Hooks: `goddessPlan()` at the start of `endWeek`, `goddessAfter(R)` after the houses run, `goddessGuard(h)` in `pushyCheck`, `goddessRebirth()` at the end of `ngBegin`, `goddessIntro()` in `setupBegin`, `goddessHouseNote()` on house purchase, and `walkGoddessEvent()` on walks.

## Forms
She has one form per race and per beastkin sub (12) (`goddess.json → forms`: build, body, skin, features; beastkin needs `sub`). `look` holds what never changes: hair, eyes, a signature feature (a lantern-flame at her shoulder, gold markings) and her outfit, so she's recognizable in every form. Each form is its own NPC (`npc_goddess_<race>`, so images are saved per form). Story scenes before the binding use `trueForm`. After it you pick a form on her tab or let her change each week (`S.goddess.shape`, 'auto' rolls `autoShape` in `goddessPlan`).
Her nights with the ladies show on their report pages with a picture: scene `pair:<service>:<fem|futa>:<her npc id>` (poses `services.json → image.fem/futa`, tags `scenes.json → people.pair_fem/pair_futa`).
